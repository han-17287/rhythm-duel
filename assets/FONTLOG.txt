PERFECTO UI, 2026-11-11

A subset of Noto Sans KR (Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License 1.1).
Original source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanskr
Copyright and license: OFL.txt in this directory.

This modified font is renamed PERFECTO UI. It retains all modern Hangul
syllables and Jamo, Latin letters, and commonly used UI symbols, including
punctuation and arrows. Original weight variation is retained; the font is losslessly compressed as WOFF2. No outlines
were redrawn. Regenerate with tools/subset_ui_font.py and an original
NotoSansKR[wght].ttf source file. FontTools and Brotli are developer-only dependencies.
